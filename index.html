Track Architect

A railway timing puzzle for iPhone and iPad

Need help? Email 4ml8kamw5@mozmail.com, or send us a message on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include your device (for example iPhone 16) and, if it's about a puzzle, the level and task number.

How to play

Drag from a train's station to lay its track, one square at a time, to the station ringed in its color. When every train has a route, tap Go. Every train must arrive without crashing, and trains sharing a station must arrive far enough apart.

Questions

A task seems impossible.
Every task has a solution. Tap the light-bulb button to watch it; that run earns 1 star, so you can try again for 3.
What's the difference between Mixed Speeds and Same Speed?
In Mixed Speeds, Locals, Expresses and Bullets each run at their own speed. In Same Speed, every train is an Express, so only route length decides who gets where first. Switch at the top of the Puzzles page.
How do I share a board I made?
Open the Workshop, choose your board and tap Share. Friends who have Track Architect can open the picture's QR code or the link to add it.
Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Stars, saved tracks and boards are stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.
Does the app collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
