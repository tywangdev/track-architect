Privacy Policy

Track Architect · Last updated October 5, 2026

Track Architect does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, saved tracks, settings, the boards you make and the name you enter are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing boards

When you share a board, you choose how to send it, for example with Messages or AirDrop. The board, and your name if you added one, go only to the people you send it to. We never receive it.

Photos

If you save a board picture, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It never browses your photo library. Import Picture, in the full game's Workshop, opens only the one picture you choose, reads the board's QR code from it, and doesn't keep the picture.

No tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no network connection of its own.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
